# Audit Severity

Use one severity per issue and repair the highest severity first.

## BLOCK

Do not deliver until repaired: unsupported product or numeric facts, identity fabrication, fake first-person experience or evidence, unauthorized claims, causal overreach, CTA permission breach, compliance-critical risk, or invariant failure after H2.

## REPAIR

Repair by default: incomplete seed/sell purpose, platform mismatch, weak commercial path, template repetition, obvious AI phrasing, over-complete structure, poor information order, or cross-version collision.

## ADVISORY

Optional improvement only: rhythm, word choice, aesthetic preference, or alternate hook when the current version is already usable. Do not apply an advisory change that introduces a new fact, weakens purpose, or makes the copy less natural.

## Resolution

Audit output records `severity`, `gate`, `evidence`, and `repair`. A release has zero BLOCK issues. REPAIR issues are either fixed or explicitly justified. ADVISORY items never block delivery.
